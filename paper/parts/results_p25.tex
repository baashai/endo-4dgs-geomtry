To test whether the FoV and initialisation findings transfer, we repeat the main configurations on StereoMIS P2\_5, frames 5301--5499 at every second frame, a clip also used by Endo-PairGS~\cite{yu2026endopairgs}. Its rectified focal length is 559.9\,px (FoV 59.5$^\circ$), and the released-style preprocessing again stores the unrectified 1033.9\,px. The camera is essentially static: the largest distance between any two camera centres is 0.12\,mm, so triangulation has no parallax and the scale of Section~\ref{sec:scale} cannot be estimated. We therefore report MoGe-2 initialisation unscaled, which uses only monocular inputs, and, as a separate stereo-assisted protocol, with a scale fitted to stereo on training frames ($\times$0.0572; MoGe-2 depth is 17.8$\times$ too large on the test frames, Table~\ref{tab:priors}).

Table~\ref{tab:p25} reproduces the FoV and initialisation findings of P1. The FoV fix adds 1.19\,dB PSNR without changing the scale-aligned depth error (0.064 vs.\ 0.063), and MoGe-2 initialisation reduces it by 28\,\% relative to FoV-corrected DA (0.064 to 0.046) and raises $\delta{<}1.25$ to 0.996, with or without scale correction. Without scaling, PSNR and SSIM match FoV-corrected DA but LPIPS is worse (0.201); the stereo-fitted scale gives the best values on all three image metrics, 1.96\,dB PSNR above the reproduced baseline. We did not run the objective, normal-loss or pose ablations on P2\_5, so the conclusions about depth supervision and poses rest on P1 alone. Endo-PairGS reports 29.06\,dB for Endo-4DGS on this clip~\cite{yu2026endopairgs}, 2.4\,dB above our reproduction; we cannot reconcile this from the published protocol description and compare only within our protocol.
