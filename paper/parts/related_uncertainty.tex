
\subsection{Uncertainty in radiance-field reconstruction}
\label{sec:related_unc}
Uncertainty for neural radiance fields and Gaussian splatting is evaluated against different targets. Several works rank \emph{depth} error with sparsification curves (AUSE) on static, non-medical scenes: CF-NeRF~\cite{shen2022cfnerf} models a distribution over radiance fields, Bayes' Rays~\cite{goli2023bayesrays} adds post-hoc spatial uncertainty to a trained NeRF, FisherRF~\cite{jiang2023fisherrf} uses the Fisher information of Gaussian parameters, also for active view selection, and training-free estimators derive it from rendering moments~\cite{ewen2025moments}, which includes the per-ray depth variance we also test, or from multi-view warping consistency~\cite{safadoust2025warprf}. Learned combinations also exist: PRIMU~\cite{gottwald2025primu} aggregates primitive-level error and coverage features by pixel-wise regression trained on held-out views, for colour and depth uncertainty, and reports generalisation to unseen scenes without further held-out data. Klasson \emph{et al.}~\cite{klasson2024sources} compare ensembles, dropout, Laplace approximations and learned variance across sources of uncertainty. In surgery, UC-NeRF~\cite{guo2024ucnerf} uses multi-view-stereo depth uncertainty to guide sparse-view reconstruction, Endo-4DGS~\cite{huang2024endo4dgs} uses a confidence map to down-weight its depth prior, and Endo-NeRF++~\cite{habib2026endonerfpp} estimates colour uncertainty for a dynamic NeRF and evaluates it against image error. In sparse-view CT, Zhao \emph{et al.}~\cite{zhao2026variance} show that Gaussian-splatting variance can correlate with error globally yet fail to localise it. For monocular depth, uncertainty is benchmarked against depth error with AUSE~\cite{poggi2020uncertainty}, including in colonoscopy~\cite{rodriguez2021colonoscopy}. Among the works we reviewed (literature search up to 2 October 2026, covering the methods cited here), we found none that evaluates per-pixel \emph{geometric} uncertainty of deformable Gaussian splatting in endoscopy against an independent depth reference, or that tests whether the confidence map of such a model indicates where its depth is wrong.
